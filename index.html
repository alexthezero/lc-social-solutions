Frequently asked

The questions that should be answered before a sales call.

Clear expectations make it easier to know whether LC is the right fit before anyone spends time on a long discovery process.

What do I need to provide each month?

Usually access to current offers, business updates, any photos or video you already have, upcoming priorities, and timely approvals. The exact workflow is set during onboarding.

Can I approve content before it goes live?

Yes. Ongoing packages are built around an organized approval point so you can review the monthly content without chasing individual posts across messages.

Do you create photos and videos?

LC can plan concepts and edit client-supplied short-form content. On-location shooting, travel, and larger production days are separate scope unless specifically included.

Which platforms can you manage?

The package structure is designed around a limited number of primary platforms so quality stays high. Platform mix is chosen based on your audience rather than trying to be everywhere.

How soon can posting begin?

Timing depends on onboarding, account access, strategy work, and approvals. A realistic launch window is set before work begins rather than promising instant posting on day one.

What is included in community management?

Light or proactive routines can include comment monitoring, response guidance, and engagement workflows. Customer-service coverage, high-volume inboxes, or around-the-clock monitoring require separate scope.

Are ads included in the monthly price?

No. Paid-social management can be added separately, and advertising spend is always paid directly by the client in addition to management fees.
